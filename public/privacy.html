JobPulse stores your account email, profile details you provide, preferences, application statuses, matching results, invitations you issue, and documents you upload. Vacancies are a shared catalog; candidate information belongs to each account.

Supabase provides account authentication, database and private file storage. Profile matching runs locally in your browser and submits a numeric profile vector to the private database. Matching results are automated suggestions, not hiring decisions. You can adjust your criteria.

Operational error reporting, when enabled by the site operator, sends generic error types and bundled code locations to Sentry. The application strips user identifiers, messages, URLs, requests, headers, breadcrumbs and arbitrary context. It does not enable session replay or performance tracing.

Access, export and deletion

Use Data and privacy or your Profile to export your profile, statuses, evaluations, issued invitations and document metadata as JSON. Download your document files separately from your profile. You can edit your information and delete documents in your profile. Account deletion removes your live account data and private files.

Deletion does not immediately erase existing provider logs or previously created backups. Completed local backups and operational logs have a maximum retention of one year; providers may retain logs for shorter periods. Local layout preferences remain in your browser until you clear site storage.

Storage and retention

Authentication uses browser storage to maintain your session. Language and layout preferences use local storage. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are required by the application. Private file links expire; avoid sharing exports or signed download links.

Contact the privacy address above for access, correction, erasure or questions about processing and provider retention. Optional personal information is used only for the profile and matching features you choose to use. Do not upload documents you are not entitled to share.
